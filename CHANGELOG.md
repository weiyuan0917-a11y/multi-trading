# Changelog

## Community 1.0.0

- Published the research-only Community edition.
- Added stock and option research examples, price-series backtests, and local
  Paper execution.

- Removed broker adapters, credentials, Pro authorization, billing, customer
  launchers, installer build files, and live execution routes from this tree.
